# Prioritized Golden NDA Template

*(Derived from analysis of six attorney-redlined NDAs)*

This document represents the **structural intent and legal protections**
attorneys consistently applied during redlining.

It is **NOT a template that must be copied verbatim**.

Instead, it is a **reference standard** used by the AI redlining system
to determine:

• which protections must exist\
• which clauses are optional\
• which clauses should never be inserted automatically

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Priority Definitions

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Priority                            Meaning
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **P1 -- Required Protection**       Clause must exist in some form. If
                                      missing, AI may insert or modify.

  **P2 -- Standard but Conditional**  Usually present but only add if
                                      clearly missing and relevant.

  **P3 -- Situational**               Only modify if already present. Do
                                      not insert automatically.

  **P4 -- Structural Only**           Used for comparison but never
                                      inserted automatically.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 1 --- Parties / Effective Date

**Priority: P1 -- Required**

Purpose:\
Identify the parties entering the agreement.

Typical language pattern:

> This Confidentiality and Nondisclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made
> as of \[date\] by and between \[Disclosing Party\] and \[Receiving
> Party\].

AI Rule:

If parties are already defined → do not modify wording.\
Only edit if parties are unclear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 2 --- Purpose of Disclosure

**Priority: P1 -- Required**

Purpose:\
Explain why confidential information is being shared.

Typical language:

> The Disclosing Party may disclose confidential information for the
> purpose of evaluating a potential transaction involving certain real
> property.

AI Rule:

If a purpose clause exists → leave it.\
Only add if the document has no purpose statement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 3 --- Definition of Confidential Information

**Priority: P1 -- Required**

Purpose:\
Define the scope of protected information.

Common elements:

• financial information\
• property data\
• transaction materials\
• operational information

AI Rule:

If definition exists → adjust wording only if extremely narrow.\
Do not expand lists unnecessarily.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 4 --- Confidentiality Obligation

**Priority: P1 -- Required**

Purpose:\
Establish the receiving party's duty to protect the information.

Typical language:

> The Receiving Party shall maintain the Confidential Information in
> strict confidence.

AI Rule:

If a confidentiality obligation exists → leave unchanged.\
Only edit if obligation is missing or unclear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 5 --- Exceptions to Confidentiality

**Priority: P1 -- Required**

Purpose:\
Prevent the NDA from being overly broad.

Standard exceptions:

• publicly available information\
• prior possession\
• third-party source\
• independent development

AI Rule:

If exceptions already exist → do not rewrite.\
Only add missing exception categories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 6 --- Permitted Recipients

**Priority: P2 -- Standard but Conditional**

Purpose:\
Allow disclosure to advisors required for transaction evaluation.

Typical recipients:

• attorneys\
• accountants\
• lenders\
• investors\
• consultants

AI Rule:

If advisor disclosure already allowed → leave unchanged.\
If document forbids disclosure entirely → add limited advisor clause.\
Otherwise do not expand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 7 --- Responsibility for Advisors

**Priority: P2 -- Standard but Conditional**

Purpose:\
Ensure the receiving party is responsible for breaches by its advisors.

Typical language:

> The Receiving Party shall remain responsible for any breach by its
> representatives.

AI Rule:

Add only if advisor disclosure exists but responsibility language is
missing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 8 --- Return or Destruction of Information

**Priority: P2 -- Standard but Conditional**

Purpose:\
Allow disclosing party to recover information.

Typical language:

> Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential
> Information.

AI Rule:

Add only if the NDA contains no material disposition language.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 9 --- Compelled Disclosure

**Priority: P2 -- Standard but Conditional**

Purpose:\
Handle subpoenas or regulatory requirements.

Typical language:

> If disclosure is required by law, the Receiving Party shall provide
> notice.

AI Rule:

Add only if the NDA has no legal disclosure provision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 10 --- Term of Agreement

**Priority: P2 -- Standard but Conditional**

Purpose:\
Define duration of confidentiality obligations.

Typical pattern:

Agreement term: 1 year\
Confidentiality survival: 1 year after termination

AI Rule:

Modify only if term is missing or unreasonable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 11 --- Remedies / Injunctive Relief

**Priority: P3 -- Situational**

Purpose:\
Allow equitable remedies.

Typical language:

> Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm.

AI Rule:

If clause exists → leave unchanged.\
Do not insert if missing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 12 --- No Obligation to Transact

**Priority: P3 -- Situational**

Purpose:\
Clarify NDA does not obligate a transaction.

AI Rule:

Only modify if clause already exists.\
Do not insert.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 13 --- No Representation or Warranty

**Priority: P3 -- Situational**

Purpose:\
Limit liability for the information provided.

AI Rule:

Do not insert.\
Modify only if already present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 14 --- Governing Law

**Priority: P3 -- Situational**

Purpose:\
Define jurisdiction.

AI Rule:

Modify only if clause exists.\
Do not insert automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 15 --- Notices

**Priority: P4 -- Structural**

Purpose:\
Formal communication mechanism.

AI Rule:

Never insert automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 16 --- Entire Agreement / Miscellaneous

**Priority: P4 -- Structural**

Purpose:\
General enforceability language.

AI Rule:

Never insert automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 17 --- Counterparts / Electronic Signatures

**Priority: P4 -- Structural**

Purpose:\
Allow modern signature execution.

AI Rule:

Never insert automatically.

# Core AI Instruction

The Golden NDA represents **legal intent, not required text**.

Rules for AI redlining:

• P1 sections may be inserted if completely absent\
• P2 sections only added if clearly missing and necessary\
• P3 sections never inserted automatically\
• P4 sections are reference only

Goal:

Align the NDA with institutional protections **without expanding the
document unnecessarily**.

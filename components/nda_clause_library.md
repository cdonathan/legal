# NDA CLAUSE LIBRARY

*(Derived from analysis of six attorney-redlined commercial real estate
NDAs)*

This clause library represents the **standard clause patterns repeatedly
introduced by attorneys** across the redlined examples.\
These clauses form the **editing vocabulary** that an AI redline system
should use when aligning incoming NDAs to institutional standards.

Each clause includes:

-   **Clause ID**
-   **Clause Name**
-   **Purpose**
-   **Standard Language Pattern**
-   **When to Apply**
-   **When NOT to Apply**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_001

## Clause Name: Parties and Effective Date

**Purpose**\
Identifies the contracting parties and establishes the effective date of
the agreement.

**Standard Language Pattern**\
This Confidentiality and Nondisclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made
as of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ("Effective Date") by and between
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ("Disclosing Party") and \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
("Receiving Party").

**When to Apply** - Always required.

**When NOT to Apply** - Never omit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_002

## Clause Name: Purpose of Disclosure

**Purpose**\
Defines the business context and limits the use of information.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Disclosing Party is willing to
disclose certain confidential information to the Receiving Party for the
purpose of evaluating a potential transaction involving certain real
property or related assets (the "Purpose").

**When to Apply** - Always present in real estate NDAs.

**When NOT to Apply** - Never omit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_003

## Clause Name: Definition of Confidential Information

**Purpose**\
Defines the scope of protected information.

**Standard Language Pattern** "Confidential Information" means any
non-public financial, operational, legal, strategic, or business
information relating to the Disclosing Party or the potential
transaction disclosed to the Receiving Party.

**Common Inclusions** - financial statements - rent rolls - leases -
property information - legal documents - analyses or summaries derived
from information

**When to Apply** - Always required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_004

## Clause Name: Derived Information Protection

**Purpose** Protects notes, analyses, and summaries created by the
recipient.

**Standard Language Pattern** Confidential Information includes
analyses, summaries, notes, or reports prepared by the Receiving Party
that contain or are derived from the Confidential Information.

**When to Apply** - When the NDA includes financial or deal materials.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_005

## Clause Name: Confidentiality Obligation

**Purpose** Establishes the recipient's duty to protect information.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Receiving Party shall maintain the
Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall protect such
information using at least the same degree of care used to protect its
own confidential information.

**When to Apply** - Always required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_006

## Clause Name: Permitted Recipients

**Purpose** Allows sharing with advisors required for deal evaluation.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Receiving Party may disclose
Confidential Information to its directors, officers, employees,
affiliates, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, consultants,
lenders, investors, or other professional advisors who have a need to
know such information for the Purpose.

**When to Apply** - Always included in modern NDAs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_007

## Clause Name: Recipient Responsibility

**Purpose** Makes the receiving party liable for advisor breaches.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Receiving Party shall remain
responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Permitted
Recipients.

**When to Apply** - Always used with Permitted Recipients clause.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_008

## Clause Name: Confidentiality Exceptions

**Purpose** Limits scope of confidential information.

**Standard Language Pattern** Confidential Information does not include
information that:

1.  becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement\
2.  was previously known by the Receiving Party\
3.  is received from a third party without confidentiality obligation\
4.  is independently developed by the Receiving Party

**When to Apply** - Always present in institutional NDAs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_009

## Clause Name: Use Limitation

**Purpose** Prevents use outside the intended deal.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Receiving Party shall use the
Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other
purpose.

**When to Apply** - Always required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_010

## Clause Name: Return or Destruction of Information

**Purpose** Ensures confidential materials are not retained.

**Standard Language Pattern** Upon written request, the Receiving Party
shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information.

**Common Exception** Archived computer backups may be retained if still
subject to confidentiality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_011

## Clause Name: Compelled Disclosure

**Purpose** Handles legal or regulatory disclosure.

**Standard Language Pattern** If disclosure is required by law or court
order, the Receiving Party shall provide prompt notice and cooperate in
seeking protective treatment.

**When to Apply** - Always present in institutional NDAs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_012

## Clause Name: Term of Agreement

**Purpose** Limits the duration of the NDA.

**Standard Language Pattern** This Agreement shall remain in effect for
one (1) year from the Effective Date.

**Typical Survival** Confidentiality obligations survive for one year
after termination.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_013

## Clause Name: Equitable Remedies

**Purpose** Allows injunction for breach.

**Standard Language Pattern** Unauthorized disclosure may cause
irreparable harm and the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief in
addition to other remedies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_014

## Clause Name: No Obligation to Transact

**Purpose** Prevents NDA from implying a deal commitment.

**Standard Language Pattern** Nothing in this Agreement obligates either
Party to enter into any transaction or business relationship.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_015

## Clause Name: No Representation or Warranty

**Purpose** Protects seller from liability regarding information
accuracy.

**Standard Language Pattern** The Disclosing Party makes no
representation or warranty regarding the accuracy or completeness of the
Confidential Information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_016

## Clause Name: Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**Purpose** Defines the legal venue.

**Standard Language Pattern** This Agreement shall be governed by the
laws of the state in which the property associated with the transaction
is located.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_017

## Clause Name: Notices

**Purpose** Defines how formal communications are delivered.

**Standard Language Pattern** All notices shall be in writing and
delivered via courier, certified mail, personal delivery, or electronic
mail with confirmation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_018

## Clause Name: Entire Agreement

**Purpose** Prevents reliance on prior statements.

**Standard Language Pattern** This Agreement constitutes the entire
agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_019

## Clause Name: Amendments

**Purpose** Controls contract modification.

**Standard Language Pattern** This Agreement may only be amended in
writing signed by both Parties.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CLAUSE: CLAUSE_020

## Clause Name: Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

**Purpose** Allows modern signature execution.

**Standard Language Pattern** This Agreement may be executed in
counterparts and electronic signatures shall be deemed valid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

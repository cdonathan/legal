# GOLD STANDARD NDA (COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT)

This **Confidentiality and Nondisclosure Agreement** ("Agreement") is
made as of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ ("Effective Date") by and between
**\[Disclosing Party\]**, with its principal place of business at
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, and **\[Receiving Party\]**,
with its principal place of business at
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (each a "Party" and
collectively the "Parties").

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_001

### Clause Name: Purpose

**Purpose:**\
Defines why confidential information is being shared and the business
context for disclosure.

**Standard Language:**\
The Disclosing Party is willing to disclose certain confidential and
proprietary information to the Receiving Party solely for the purpose of
evaluating a potential transaction involving certain real property,
assets, or related business operations (the "Purpose").

**Key Requirements** - Must define the potential transaction context. -
Must clarify disclosure is limited to evaluation of the transaction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_002

### Clause Name: Definition of Confidential Information

**Purpose:**\
Defines the scope of protected information.

**Standard Language:**\
"Confidential Information" means any non‑public financial, operational,
legal, strategic, or business information relating to the Disclosing
Party or the potential transaction that is disclosed to the Receiving
Party, whether orally, electronically, visually, or in written form.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

-   financial statements\
-   rent rolls\
-   leases\
-   operating statements\
-   tenant information\
-   legal documentation\
-   property information\
-   transaction materials\
-   analyses, summaries, notes, or reports derived from such information

Confidential Information also includes the existence of discussions
between the Parties regarding the potential transaction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_003

### Clause Name: Exceptions to Confidential Information

**Purpose:**\
Prevents overly broad NDA enforcement.

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

1.  is publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;\
2.  was already in the Receiving Party's possession without
    confidentiality restriction;\
3.  is obtained from a third party not under confidentiality
    obligations;\
4.  is independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_004

### Clause Name: Confidentiality Obligations

**Purpose:**\
Establishes duty of care and use limitations.

The Receiving Party shall:

-   maintain the Confidential Information in strict confidence
-   use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose
-   protect such information with at least the same degree of care used
    to protect its own confidential information, and no less than
    reasonable care.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_005

### Clause Name: Permitted Recipients

**Purpose:**\
Allows disclosure to advisors necessary for deal evaluation.

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its:

-   directors
-   officers
-   employees
-   affiliates
-   partners
-   members
-   managers
-   attorneys
-   accountants
-   financial advisors
-   consultants
-   prospective lenders
-   equity partners
-   investors

(collectively **"Permitted Recipients"**) who have a need to know such
information for the Purpose.

The Receiving Party shall remain responsible for any breach by its
Permitted Recipients.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_006

### Clause Name: Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall
promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, archival records and automatic system
backups may be retained if they remain subject to confidentiality
obligations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_007

### Clause Name: Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law or court order to disclose
Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

1.  provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (where legally
    permitted);\
2.  cooperate in seeking protective treatment of such information.

Only the portion legally required shall be disclosed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_008

### Clause Name: Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for **one (1) year** from the
Effective Date.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive for **one (1) year after
termination or expiration** of the Agreement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_009

### Clause Name: Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure may cause
irreparable harm.

Accordingly, the Disclosing Party may seek:

-   injunctive relief
-   equitable remedies
-   damages or other remedies available at law.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_010

### Clause Name: No Obligation to Complete Transaction

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into a
transaction.

Either Party may discontinue discussions at any time without liability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_011

### Clause Name: No Representation or Warranty

The Disclosing Party makes **no representation or warranty** regarding
the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information.

The Receiving Party is responsible for its own due diligence and
evaluation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_012

### Clause Name: Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the
subject property or transaction is located, without regard to
conflict‑of‑law principles.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_013

### Clause Name: Notices

All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by:

-   personal delivery
-   nationally recognized courier
-   certified mail
-   electronic mail with confirmation of receipt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_014

### Clause Name: Entire Agreement / Miscellaneous

This Agreement:

-   constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
-   supersedes all prior discussions
-   may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties
-   binds successors and permitted assigns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CLAUSE_ID: NDA_015

### Clause Name: Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

Electronic signatures shall be deemed valid and binding.

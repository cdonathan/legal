Consideration .

Purchase Price . The Purchase Price shall be payable in full in readily available funds at the Closing.

Earnest Money . In order to secure Buyer’s performance of this Agreement, Buyer shall, on or before the expiration of 5 business days after the Effective Date, deposit with the Title Company the Earnest Money. The Earnest Money shall be placed in an interest bearing account and any interest earned thereon shall become a part of the Earnest Money for all purposes. If this transaction closes, the Earnest Money shall be applied against the Purchase Price at Closing. If this Agreement has not been terminated, the Earnest Money shall be nonrefundable to Buyer after the last day of the Inspection Period, except as otherwise provided in this Agreement.

Title and Survey .

Title Commitment . Within 10 days after the Effective Date, Seller shall furnish to Buyer a standard TLTA title commitment (the “Title Commitment”) issued by the Title Company showing Seller as the record fee title owner of the Land by the terms of which the Title Company agrees to issue to Buyer at Closing an owner’s policy of title insurance as hereafter specified (“Title Policy”) together with legible copies of all exceptions referred to in the Title Commitment (“Title Review Documents”). The Title Policy shall (i) be in the amount of the Purchase Price or such greater amount that Buyer may request, provided that Buyer pays for the additional premium, (ii) contain no exception for “parties in possession”, “tenants under unrecorded leases”, or any other standard printed exceptions that may be deleted under title insurance rules in the state where the Land is located, and (iii) have, at Buyer’s sole expense, such additional coverages or endorsements over the base Owner’s Policy as Buyer may require.

Survey . Within 10 days after the Effective Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a copy of Seller’s most recent ALTA survey of the Property, if any. Buyer may obtain a new or updated ALTA survey of the Property (the “Survey”). Seller shall reimburse Buyer for the cost of the Survey at Closing or upon an earlier termination of this Agreement, if terminated due to a Seller default. Unless the Land is a platted lot, upon approval of the Survey by Buyer, the metes and bounds description of the Land in the Survey shall automatically be substituted for the description of the Land on Exhibit A to this Agreement and at Buyer’s election will be used in the Deed (defined below) and any other documents requiring a legal description of the Land.

On or before 20 days after the Title Commitment, legible copies of the Title Review Documents, and the Survey have all been delivered to Buyer, Buyer may provide Seller with written notice of any title or survey objections (“Title Objections”). If Seller is unable or unwilling to cause the removal of any of the Title Objections within 5 business days after delivery of Buyer’s notice of Title Objections, Seller may notify Buyer in writing, and Buyer may, on or before the day that is 5 business days after the later of receipt of said notice or the deadline for the delivery of said notice: (a) terminate this Agreement by giving Seller written notice of termination, whereupon the Earnest Money shall be immediately returned to Buyer or (b) waive, in writing, any uncured Title Objections. If Buyer fails to timely notify Seller of its election in the preceding sentence, Buyer shall be deemed to have elected subpart (b). The term “Permitted Exceptions” means (a) all exceptions reflected on standard [Schedule B of the Title Commitment which are not timely objected to by Buyer except for those exceptions that the Title Company subsequently removes from a Title Commitment, (b) any Title Objections that are waived or deemed waived by Buyer, except for (x) those exceptions that the Title Company subsequently removes from a Title Commitment and (y) the Mandatory Cure Items and (c) any Retained Property Easements (defined below). Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in this Agreement or in any response by Seller to Title Objections Seller shall, without necessity of objection by Buyer, (i) deliver to the Title Company at Closing an owner’s affidavit with such information that the Title Company reasonably requests, (ii) discharge all liens encumbering title to the Property, excluding liens for property taxes that are not yet due and payable, and (iii) satisfy all Seller requirements requested by the Title Company on the Title Commitment ((i)-(iii) are collectively referred to herein as the “Mandatory Cure Items”). Seller shall keep the Title Commitment in full force and effect throughout the term of this Agreement.

Additional Title Objections . In the event that any additional adverse matters are disclosed by or contained in the Survey or any update of the Title Commitment or Survey, Buyer shall have 10 days after receipt of the Survey or such updated Title Commitment or Survey to object to such matters. Any such objection will be considered a Title Objection and will be subject to the same cure requirements, time deadlines for cure, and remedies for failure to cure as are set forth above with respect to Title Objections, except that each time period for curative action with respect to any additional Title Objections will commence on the date of Buyer’s notice to Seller raising such additional Title Objections.
